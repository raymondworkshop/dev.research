[AI Synthesis]: Charm in talk means putting your ego aside, steering with open questions, and keeping the exchange meaningful to the other person — you learn more about them than they learn about you.

### Ego back burner
* Putting your ego on the back burner and attending to the other person’s needs or interests is the key to being charming.
* Focus on making others feel liked and popular.
* Keep the conversation bouncing back to the other person; use questions that spin off from what you’re already talking about.

### Steer with questions
* Steer any conversation with who, what, why, when, where, and how.
* Charm by keeping the conversation meaningful to that person — a chance to be creative while focusing on their needs.
* Soft redirect: a twenty- or thirty-degree shift (e.g. slip in your own appreciation of dogs) rather than a hard turn.
* Begin with polite social banter so both sides can gauge mood and receptivity.

### Preparation and depth
* Be completely focused on the other person.
* The more you learn about others in conversation, the greater your potential influence — ask better questions.
* Goal: participate actively while encouraging them to take over as much as they want.
* Sample ladder: “What sort of work do you do?” → “How did you get into that line of work?” → “And then what did you do?” → “Tell me more about that.” / “How did you feel?” / “What happened next?”

### Actions
* Be clear about your intent: charming.
* Avoid conflict at all costs; present your point of view gently, tentatively, then let it go.
* Only take over when others make it clear they want to hear from you.
* Use a well-placed question; know more about them than they know about you.
* Slow down; use silences — fillers (aah, umm, uh, er) disappear when you allow silence.
* Tape yourself reading aloud; become a better speaker by pausing a second or two.
