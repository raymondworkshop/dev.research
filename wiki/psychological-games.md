[AI Synthesis]: Psychological games are patterned social exchanges with a hidden payoff. Players repeat them for strokes and structure; the practical difference between skilled and unskilled participants is knowing when to stop.

### What distinguishes skilled players
* In any game, the gap between professionals and amateurs is simple: professionals know when to quit.

### Common player types
* The sulker is someone angry at mother—Parent/Child material kept in play rather than resolved.
* The "weirdo" is overly sensitive to Parent ego-state influence—social oddness as a defense against scripted norms.

### Actions
* When a recurring argument or mood loop feels familiar, ask what game is running and what payoff each side is collecting.
* Practice stopping earlier than the script demands—treat quitting as skill, not defeat.
