[AI Synthesis]: Choose role models by how they approach the work. Accomplishment, an ego that stays in check, and a life that still has health and relationships are the test. Pay is one part of compensation.

### Motivations and mindsets
* Effectiveness as a leader depends in part on understanding people’s motivations and mindsets.
* Ask what they like most about their jobs.
* Look for an accomplishment orientation: ego in check, good work every day, with fame and money left out of the center.

### Health and relationships stay in the picture
* Intrinsic motivation and big goals work when mental and physical health, a sense of balance, strong family bonds, and positive, productive working relationships stay intact.

### A wider compensation
* Pay at competitive levels and differentiate on performance.
* Count autonomy, power, prestige, the social aspects of the work, and altruism as part of what the job pays.

### Actions
* Ask someone what they like most about the job, and listen for accomplishment, ego, and external reward.
* When a result tempts you as a model, check whether health, family, and working relationships are still there.
