[AI Synthesis]: Meaning is a goal you pursue, carried by how you define the work. Leaders make it concrete as direction and a mission people can take seriously.

### Emphasize the why
* Emphasize your why.
* Ask the big questions. You may find the work has more purpose than you thought.
* How you choose to define the work changes what the work is for the person doing it.

### Direction
* Leaders who carry this set big goals. They create a sense of direction. They focus on a worthy mission.

### Actions
* Ask what the work is for, in a sentence you would still mean on a hard week.
* When you set a goal, name the mission it serves.
