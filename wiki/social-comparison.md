[AI Synthesis]: Social comparison is a motivator, often stronger than encouragement. The same highlights keep it inside collaboration: gratitude for what the team has, and a contest that leaves trust, respect, and the mission standing. Mastery still defines accomplishment; competition is a tool beside it.

### Want less of what you lack
* Social comparison is a powerful motivator for individuals and organizations.
* It is in your power to release the wish for what you do not have, and to put what you have to good use.
* Teams rarely spend enough time on what makes them great, on the advantages they are lucky to have, or on why the culture is special. In hard times especially, gratitude lifts morale.

### Competition that serves the team
* Keep internal competition secondary to collaboration, or turn it into a tool for collaboration.
* Make internal competition friendly and enjoyable.
* It has to leave trust, mutual respect, and a shared understanding of the team’s mission in place.
* Rally the team against a worthy competitor.
* Most people get more motivation from competition than from positive encouragement.

### Actions
* Name a worthy external competitor and the mission the comparison serves.
* In a hard week, say what already makes the team and the culture worth having.
* If an internal contest would spend trust, change the contest so collaboration is what people are trying to win.
