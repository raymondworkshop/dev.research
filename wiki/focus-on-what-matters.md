[AI Synthesis]: Signal versus noise is a longer horizon. Stay near revenue and near human capital you can keep, communicate, do a few things at a high standard, and treat diet, exercise, and sleep as the job of a corporate athlete.

### The long view
* Separate signal from noise by taking a long-term perspective.
* Focus on the trend. Slight variations around it are noise.
* Assess your performance and the business over longer horizons. Ask what the progress has been over the last three years and the last five.
* Keep the issues that will affect your ability to achieve your goals over time. Treat the rest as noise and read progress on the longer view.
* Expectations shape happiness. Manage them with care.

### A mentor
* Find a mentor you can talk to about your challenges: someone with experience and perspective.
* Ask for a clinical, unbiased assessment of the challenge.

### Stay close to revenue
* Get closer to clients. Learn their needs and make the work relevant to the firm’s commercial success.
* Ask where the revenue stream is, and how to get closer to it.

### Human capital
* Build human capital: what differentiates you and what no one can take away. A network of industry contacts, publications, a reputation on the conference circuit, credentials.
* Align that effort so it stays relevant to the job. Employers want you to build it.
* Keep a line of your own progress: creating, innovating, leading, selling, or taking risk.

### Communication and a few things done well
* Emphasize communication. Collaboration and teamwork are essential in corporate life. On the job, as much as half of success can turn on how well you communicate.
* Write weekly updates. Speak clearly in meetings and presentations.
* Pay attention to details. Check the work more than once.
* Do a few things very well, and check them with care. Too many projects at a “just ok” standard dilute the work.

### The corporate athlete
* Mind diet, exercise, and sleep. Think of yourself as a corporate athlete.
* Build habits.
* Mindfulness and meditation belong in that training.

### Actions
* Keep a worry that changes the three- to five-year goal. Set the others down and look at the trend.
* Ask a mentor for an unbiased read of one challenge.
* Name the revenue stream and the client need this work serves.
* Do fewer things this week, check them carefully, and protect sleep, food, and exercise.
