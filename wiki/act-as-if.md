[AI Synthesis]: Charm from the outside in: people judge behavior. Act as if you already are charming (or already feel the emotion you want); outward action triggers matching inner feeling.

### Outside in vs inside out
* **What matters is what people think you are**.
* “Inside out” (American) vs “**outside in**” (European): **change external behavior with skills** that control the image others see.
* Behave as though you are happy or excited and others (and often you) will feel it.
* Worry less about the why; **concentrate on the how** — often easier and faster to change.
* **Concentrate on how you can behave** differently, not why you do things.

### Behavior is everything
* You are ultimately judged by your behavior.
* People react to how they perceive you to behave, whatever your inner agenda.
* Concentrate on behaving exactly as if you were already a charming person.

### Actions trigger feelings
* Pretend as if you already feel it until it becomes reality.
* Outward behavior creates the corresponding inner feeling; controlling actions helps create the emotions you want.
* Example: dress and act “well” and midmorning you feel better — behaviors of being well.

### Actions
* Build skills that change external behavior and the personal image others see.
* Create a mental image of yourself as charming inside — then act accordingly outside.
* Act as if you already felt a particular way; actions soon trigger matching feelings.
