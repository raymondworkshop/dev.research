[AI Synthesis]: People structure time to meet three hungers—stimulation, recognition (strokes), and structure—and fill the gaps with procedures, rituals, or games until intimacy offers a fuller answer.

### Strokes and social recognition
* The form of stimulation people most prefer is physical intimacy.
* A stroke is the recognition one person gives another—the basic unit of social acknowledgment.

### Three hungers
* After the hunger for stimulation and the hunger for recognition comes the hunger for structure.
* Stimulation and recognition ward off sensory and emotional deprivation.
* Structure expresses the need to avoid boredom.
* Intimacy is the only answer that can fully satisfy all three; its prototype is loving sexual union.

### Procedures and rituals
* A procedure is a series of simple complementary Adult-to-Adult exchanges; the Adult decides how it runs.
* A ritual is a fixed series of simple complementary exchanges governed by Parent-like rules and norms.

### Actions
* Notice which hunger a conversation or habit is really feeding—stimulation, stroke, or structure—and whether a ritual has replaced genuine contact.
* Prefer direct Adult exchanges (procedures) when the goal is information; recognize when Parent-scripted rituals are carrying the social load.
