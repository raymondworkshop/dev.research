### Mastery and the wish to win
* The psyche combines a drive toward mastery and a desire to win.
* Separate winning at all costs from a broader notion of accomplishment.
* Define accomplishment as a function of mastery goals.

### The process
* Mastery is about process. It means making how you do things better.
* Play to improve, to learn, to solve problems, and to be in flow.
* When they win, it is great. When they lose, as long as they played well, it is almost as great.
* The standard in the story is the time you played to the absolute best of your abilities.

### Two ways of playing
* The mastery-focused player enjoys the game more and is better equipped to cope with mistakes. The aim is to improve the swing, and the satisfaction comes from that. This player plays for the sake of playing.
* The other player wants to impress others with a low handicap, and plays for the sake of winning.

### Actions
* After a win or a loss, ask whether you played well and whether the way of doing it improved.
* For one session, define the aim as improving a skill you can feel in the work itself.
