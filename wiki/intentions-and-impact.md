[AI Synthesis]: Don’t assume you know what they meant. Speak to impact; inquire about intentions. Good intentions do not erase bad impact — and intentions are often more complex than good or bad.

### Don’t assume you know their intentions
* We attribute intentions based on the impact on us.
* We assume the worst; we assume bad intentions mean bad character; we treat ourselves more charitably.
* Speak to the impact; inquire about their intentions.
* Follow the action–impact–assumption path: our assumptions are guesses, subject to modification — share them as such, then hear what the other person says.

### Good intentions do not erase bad impact
* Assuming good intentions erase bad impact changes the topic of the conversation.
* Intentions are often more complex than just “good” or “bad.”
* Listen for feelings, and reflect on your intentions.

### Actions
* Name the impact on you; ask about their intentions instead of asserting them.
* Share your assumption as a guess open to disproof.
* When they claim good intent, still address impact; listen for feelings and check your own intentions.
