[AI Synthesis]: Agreeableness is a strength: the willingness to go along. Leadership also needs a small share of disagreeableness, aimed at necessary decisions and at helping people grow, delivered so they still feel valued.

### Go along, and sometimes refuse
* Agreeableness is the willingness to go along and get along, without having to have it your way.
* Balance that with the courage to make unpopular decisions the situation requires.
* If you are too agreeable, some people will take advantage of you. A bit of disagreeableness has advantages.
* Be disagreeable about 10% of the time. Learn to pick your battles.
* You push people to grow when the feedback is respectful and lets them know they are valued.

### Actions
* Before you yield, decide whether this is one of the few battles worth the disagreement.
* When you disagree, give the feedback respectfully and make it clear the person is valued.
