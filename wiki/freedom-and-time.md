[AI Synthesis]: Money’s highest dividend is control over your time. Independence and autonomy from unspent assets matter more than many luxury purchases.

### Control over time
* People want to control their lives.
* The ability to do what you want, when you want, with who you want, for as long as you want, is priceless.
* A strong sense of controlling one’s life is a more dependable predictor of positive wellbeing than many financial metrics.

### Money buys time and options
* Money’s greatest intrinsic value is its ability to give you control over your time.
* Unspent assets buy independence and autonomy—greater control over what you can do and when.
* Using money to buy time and options has a lifestyle benefit few luxury goods can match.

### What people actually value
* Quality friendships, belonging to something bigger than yourself, and unstructured time with children often matter more than conspicuous spending.

### Highest dividend in finance
* The ability to do what you want, when you want, with who you want, for as long as you want pays the highest dividend that exists in finance.

### Actions
* When spending, compare the time and option value of a purchase against its status value.
* Use savings to widen the gap between what you must do and what you choose to do.
