[AI Synthesis]: Berne's structural analysis holds that every person carries Parent, Adult, and Child ego states—coherent bundles of thinking, feeling, and behavior—and shifts among them according to time and circumstance.

### Three ego states
* At any moment in a social setting, a person operates from Parent, Adult, or Child, and can move between them with varying degrees of willingness.
* Once an ego state is recognized, it becomes the lens for reading what someone is doing and why.

### Parent
* "That's your Parent ego state" means your mind is acting like a parent figure from the past—same mannerisms, posture, vocabulary, and feelings.
* Parent guidance is largely unconscious, which saves time and energy in routine social life.

### Adult
* "That's your Adult ego state" means you assess the situation objectively and respond without prejudice—stating your reasoning, what you notice, or your conclusions.
* Adult is necessary for survival: it processes data and calculates how to handle the external world.
* Another Adult task is to constrain Parent and Child and mediate between them; satisfaction comes when that regulation succeeds.

### Child
* "That's your Child ego state" means you react with the behavior and motives you would have had as a young boy or girl.
* Child is the most precious part of personality—charm, joy, and creativity; intuition, spontaneity, play, and fun live here.
* Everyone has a little boy or girl inside; Child contributes to life the way a child contributes to a family.

### Actions
* When a reaction feels automatic or over-familiar, name which ego state is running before deciding how to respond.
* Use Adult to check whether Parent scripts or Child impulses fit the present situation.
