[AI Synthesis]: Open from the third story — a joint description neither side owns alone — then invite them in as a partner. Map forward: third story, their story, your story.

### Begin from the third story
* Your purpose in opening is to invite the other person into a joint exploration.
* “My sense is that you and I see this situation differently. I’d like to share how I’m seeing it, and learn more about how you’re seeing it.”
* “I wanted to talk about … I wanted to understand why you see things the way you do, and to share with you my perspective and feelings.”

### Extend an invitation
* Invite: offer and discuss a possible description and purpose.
* Make them your partner in figuring it out.

### Bad news and requests
* Delivering bad news: share the news up front clearly and empathetically, then invite a third-story frame — here’s the news; you have your views and reactions; I have mine; let’s make room to discuss each.
* Making requests: invite an exploration — “I’d like to explore whether a raise for me might make sense. From the information I have, I think I deserve one. [reasoning.] I wonder how you see it?”

### Map going forward
* Third story → their story → your story.
* Explore where each story comes from; share impact; take responsibility for your contribution and explore theirs; describe feelings and inquire about theirs; reflect on identity issues.

### Actions
* Open from the third story; invite joint exploration before arguing your side.
* For bad news: lead with clarity and empathy, then make room for both reactions.
* For requests: explore fairness together rather than demand.
