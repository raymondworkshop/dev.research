[AI Synthesis]: Charm signals are mostly nonverbal: eye contact, eye flicks, head tilts and nods, open whole-body orientation, vocal and verbal reassurances. Delivery outweighs words (body ~55%, tone ~38%, words ~7%).

### Eye contact and the flick
* Make direct, focused eye contact — especially when listening.
* Eyes flick eye-to-eye (or eyes–lips triangle); more eye activity reads as more engaged.
* Observe people; practice shifting gaze between their eyes.
* Look-aside: briefly shift gaze to the side of the face / ear, then back to mouth and eyes — never more than two or three seconds.
* In one-on-one, look at the other person’s eyes no more than ~85% of the time; in groups, shift gaze gently person to person.

### Head, body, voice
* Nod while listening; tilt head to listen, straighten to speak.
* Combine vocal reassurances with eye contact and nodding (“I see,” “Really?”, “I agree, absolutely”).
* Keep the whole body turned toward the person; arms unfolded; sit upright (back off the chair).
* Soften voice and lean forward when engaged.
* Deeper vocal sounds suggest size and strength; higher sounds convey smallness and weakness.
* A smile can be heard and felt in your voice on the phone.
* You are judged by how you position your body; stay in control of the signals you send.
* Focus on delivery techniques.

### Actions
* Practice excellent, focused eye contact when listening; flick gaze eye to eye.
* Small head tilt when someone speaks; whole body oriented; arms open.
* Sit up straight; soften voice; lean in when answering.
* Gently lower your voice toward deeper, warmer tones — don’t force it; vary tone up and down.
* Slow down and use silence.
* Speak warmly and gently; speak clearly, simply, and directly.
