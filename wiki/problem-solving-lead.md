[AI Synthesis]: Take the lead without trying to control their reaction. Reframe blame into a learning conversation, embrace both stories with “and,” make trouble in the talk itself explicit, and seek fair standards or clear alternatives when you still disagree.

### Raise it without controlling their reaction
* Raise it because it matters; do it as well as you can. If they aren’t interested or open, you can still feel good that you stuck up for yourself — you are not responsible for controlling their reaction.

### Skills for leading
* Reframe from blame to a learning conversation:
  * Truth → different stories: “I want to make sure I understand your perspective. I’d also like to share mine.”
  * Accusations → intentions and impact: “It wasn’t my intention. Can you say more about how you felt?”
  * Blame → joint contribution: “I’m sure I’ve contributed; I think we both have. Rather than whose fault, I’d like to look at how we got here.”
  * Judgments → feelings: “It sounds like you’re feeling really unhappy with my behavior.”
  * What’s wrong with you → what’s going on for you: “Whether we can work together to address both of our concerns.”
* Use “and” to embrace both stories instead of arguing whose version is true.
* Listen, ask questions, try to understand how they experience the issue.
* Make the trouble explicit: put on the table what you see happening in the conversation itself — “Here’s what I’m noticing.” “I really do want to know what’s upsetting you, and I want us to find a way to talk about it that…”

### Compromise and standards
* Difficult conversations require compromise and mutual accommodation.
* Gather information and test perceptions; ask their advice; “Help me understand how you would feel if you were in my shoes.”
* Ask what standards should apply — fair principles to guide a resolution.
* If you still can’t agree, consider your alternatives: accept less than you want, or accept the consequences of not agreeing.

### Actions
* Reframe live: different stories, intentions/impact, joint contribution, feelings.
* Name process trouble when the conversation itself is stuck.
* When stuck on substance, ask for fair standards; then weigh alternatives honestly.
