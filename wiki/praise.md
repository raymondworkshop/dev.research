[AI Synthesis]: Praise and warmth raise self-esteem: thank often, smile with mood, and praise quickly, specifically, for large and small accomplishments.

### Appreciation and thank-you
* Appreciation and praise are vitally important; people track how praiseworthy they feel.
* Say thank you on every occasion, large or small — make a habit of thanking everyone for everything they do.

### Be quick to praise
* Praise elevates self-esteem.
* Be specific — a trait, possession, or accomplishment.
* Praise immediately.
* Praise both large and small accomplishments.
* Smiling and laughing is infectious — match the other person’s mood.

### Actions
* Say “thank you” on every occasion, for any large or small reason.
* Praise people for something they have done; compliment a trait, possession, or accomplishment.
* Be quick, specific, and praise small wins as well as big ones.
* Match the other person’s mood with smile and laugh.
