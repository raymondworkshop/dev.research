[AI Synthesis]: Blame judges; contribution understands. Map how everyone involved helped create the problem — not necessarily equally — then take responsibility for your share early and invite theirs.

### Blame vs contribution
* At heart, blame is about judging and contribution is about understanding.
* Recognizing that everyone involved contributed does not mean everyone contributed equally.
* As you look at how each of you contributed, sharing your feelings is essential.

### Hard-to-spot contributions
* You have allowed the problem to continue unchecked by not having addressed it earlier.
* Successful relationships rest on knowing that in intersections there is no one to blame — people are just different; staying together sometimes means compromising preferences and meeting in the middle.
* Problematic role assumptions also contribute.

### Take responsibility early
* “We’ve both contributed to this, and I can’t fix it by myself. It’s neither fair nor sustainable for us to look only at my behavior and not yours…”
* Help them see their contribution by sharing what triggered you and making a specific request for how they can change their contribution.

### Actions
* Map the joint contribution system before assigning fault.
* Name your contribution early; ask for theirs without requiring equal weight.
* Share triggers and make a specific request for change going forward.
