[AI Synthesis]: Engagement rises when the work returns a lot for the time it costs, the goal is clear, and the feedback is defined. Challenge that matches skill is where flow shows up. Randomness in business means many of those goals will be non-linear.

### Return on time
* Ask which activities yield the greatest return on your time.
* Estimate how much an activity contributes to the real aim. In the note’s example, that aim is making money for clients.
* High return on time spent is more likely to line up with long-term goals and values.
* As a leader, put meetings on the same test: keep the high-return ones.
* Find flow in the rest of life as well as at work.

### Clear goals and defined feedback
* Goals should be clear, and the rewards or feedback should be well defined.
* The business world is full of randomness, which means non-linear goals.
* SMART goals are specific, measurable, achievable, realistic, and time-based. Each of those elements supports flow.

### Actions
* List the week’s activities and keep the ones with the highest return on time toward the long-term goal.
* For one goal, write the feedback you will actually receive, and when.
