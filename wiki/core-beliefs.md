[AI Synthesis]: Core beliefs are the operating system under judgment. They show up as strong feeling. Knowing the roots, including early and memorable experience, is how a leader spends less energy on repeated errors.

### The beliefs you feel
* Know yourself better. Understand your roots and define your belief systems.
* Core beliefs are what you feel deeply.
* Explore childhood and traumatic or memorable life events when you need to.
* Managing and upgrading those beliefs helps you avoid frequent errors in judgment, allocate time and energy better, and lead better.

### Actions
* When a reaction is stronger than the situation, name the belief you are treating as fact and where it may have started.
* Decide whether that belief still deserves the time and energy it is taking.
