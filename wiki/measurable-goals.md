[AI Synthesis]: Measurable goals are how a leader aligns people and keeps score. They still sit next to process, and a result still has to be split into luck and skill.

### Why people persist
* The most successful people persist through difficulty from an internal desire to control their lives, learn about the world, and accomplish something that endures.

### What the goals are for
* The leader’s job is to set goals for the organization and for individuals.
* Measurable goals are essential in business and life. They sharpen communication, collaboration, and performance evaluation. They motivate and align an organization.
* The goal organizes and measures the best of people’s energies and skills.

### Outcome, process, and luck
* You will stress about outcomes. Also focus on what the team can control: the process.
* Differentiate luck and skill. Examine the decision-making process.
* Improve the mental game: systematize warning signals, encourage preventive behavior, and exit on stop-loss rules.

### Actions
* For each goal, name the process the team controls, alongside the outcome.
* After a result, review the decision process before you assign the outcome to skill.
* Write the warning signals and the stop-loss before you need them.
