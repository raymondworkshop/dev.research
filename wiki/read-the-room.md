### What the job actually is
* Much of the work is relationships: helping people find their motivation, consoling those who feel down about the work, and resolving conflicts.
* Empathy is the key. Listen and understand the other perspective, especially when it differs from yours. Understand why people think what they think and do what they do.
* All behavior is purposeful. When the reason is hard to see, spend time on what the behavior is for.

### A map of traits
* The traits named in the note are extraversion, agreeableness, neuroticism, openness, and conscientiousness.
* [AI Synthesis]: This highlight set develops extraversion, on this page, and agreeableness, on [[be-disagreeable]].

### Call on the introverts
* Stop the extroverts and call on the introverts.
* Apologize for interrupting, summarize the person’s point, and pivot.
* The sequence: recognize the point, apologize, name the time left, restate what they are saying, then ask a quieter person what they think.
* Listening is a superpower. Listen, then summarize well and briefly.

### Actions
* When behavior confuses you, ask what purpose it serves before you decide what it means.
* In a meeting, after a long turn, apologize, summarize in one sentence, and ask an introvert.
