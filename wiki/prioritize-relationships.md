[AI Synthesis]: Relationships are the priority that makes debate, feedback, and culture usable. People need to feel cared for as people. Culture is the behavior the group actually repeats.

### What people need from the relationship
* Positive working relationships rest on collaboration and mutual respect.
* Elite athletes value two things in a coach: the feeling of being cared for as a person, and applicable feedback that leads to improvement.
* The most important life goal is to nurture positive relationships.

### Disagree on the idea
* With clear rules of the game and genuine respect, debate the ideas.
* Before you disagree, name something you agree on: “We all agree that …, but where I see things differently is …”
* Build a culture where people ask for feedback.
* Collaboration includes being able to disagree with mutual respect.

### Culture is what you do
* Culture drives everything. It is the shared set of norms, beliefs, and expected behaviors.
* Safety, belonging, trust, and shared vulnerability are the building blocks of this kind of family culture.
* Culture is more about what you do than what you say. Communication still matters; the repeated behavior is the culture.
* When you judge a culture, ask, listen, and request examples of decisions that show it.

### Who owns what
* Keep role clarity. When people are unclear who owns what, conflicts arise.
* That can mean rethinking job definitions, reporting lines, and hiring and firing.
* Remind people they have incentives to work together.
* Deliver the best the firm can offer this client. Start from what the client needs, then recommend.

### Three measures
* If the company had to run on three measures, they would be customer satisfaction, employee satisfaction, and cash flow.

### Actions
* In the next disagreement, state the shared point, then the difference.
* Ask someone for feedback, and when culture comes up, ask for a decision that illustrates it.
* Where a conflict repeats, write down who owns what.
