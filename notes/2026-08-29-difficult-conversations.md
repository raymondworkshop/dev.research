### The Dilemma: Avoid or Confront

- [insights] Why is it so difficult to **decide whether to avoid or to confront**? Because at some level we know the truth: if we try to avoid the problem, we’ll feel taken advantage of, our feelings will fester, we’ll **wonder why we don’t stick up for ourselves**, and we’ll rob the other person of the opportunity to improve things. But if we confront the problem, **things might get even worse**. We may be rejected, attacked, or misunderstood. We might hurt the other person in ways we didn’t intend, and the relationship might suffer.

    - [info] Delivering a difficult message is like throwing a hand grenade

    - [info] At heart, the problem isn’t in your actions, it’s in your thinking

    - [info] Eliminating fear and anxiety is an unrealistic goal

    - [insights] **Achieving perfect results with no risk will not happen**

    - **Getting better results in the face of tolerable odds might.**
