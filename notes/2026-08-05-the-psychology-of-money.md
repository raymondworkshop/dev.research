## ch5 - Getting Wealthy vs Staying Wealthy  

- [info]  stay wealthy: some combination of frugality and paranoia  

- [insights] Getting money requires **taking risks**, **being optimistic**, and **putting yourself out there**.   But keeping money requires the opposite of taking risk. It requires humility, and fear that what you’ve made can be taken away from you just as fast.  

- [insights] Applying the survival mindset to the real world  
    - More than I want big returns, I want to be **financially unbreakable**  

    -   **Room for error** - often called margin of safety— is one of the most underappreciated forces in finance.  **A frugal budget, flexible thinking, and a loose timeline** — anything that lets you live happily with a range of outcomes.
