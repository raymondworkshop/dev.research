## The Psychologyof Leadership  
* That time, **I played to the absolute best of my abilities**    

*  A leader's job is to **inspire others**    
    - **You put on your oxygen mask before helping others**  
        - **Understand yourself** so you can understand others.
    It's about **empathy**, **leading by example**, and **personal resilience**.  
    - **focusing on mastery** over ego, **the importance of relationships and community**, the meaning of sport as a **meritocracy**, and engagement and flow  


### P1 - measurable goals  

* They're **working hard** and** persisting through difficulties** 
because of **their internal desire to control their lives**, **learn about the world**,
and **accomplish somethign that endures**    

* The leader's job is to **set goals for their organization and individuals**  
    - **measurable goals aer essential in business and life**    
    - goals enhance communication, collaboration, and performance evaluations. they motivate and align organizations   
    - the goal will serve to organize and measure the best of our energies and skills  

* improve your mental game  
    - **systematize warning signals**, encourage preventative behaviors, and **exit based on stop-loss rules**  
    - I **stess about the outcomes**, 
    and I **also focus on what my team can control: our process**  

    - **differentiate between luck and skill**. make sure you **examine your decision-making process**

* Happiness comes from positive emotions + Engagement/flow,  relationships, meaning, and accomplishments  
    - maintain positive relationships with people around them  
    - engage in tasks that are challenging yet match their capabilities

### P8 - Build a bigger rocket - unleash the power of social comparison  

* **Social comparison** is a powerful motivator for individuals and organizations  
    - It is in their power to **not want what they donot have**, and **to cheerfully put to good use what they have**  
        - they rarely spend enough time reminding everyong of what makes the team great,
        why the organization is lucky to benefit from competitive advantages, or why their culture is special. Even in tough times, or especially in tough times, **gratitude lifts morale**.  

*  **Internal competition should be secondary to collaboration** - or even a tool for it  
    - Make interna lcompetition friendly and enjoyable  
        - Internal competition must not come at the expense of  trust, mutual respect,
        and a shared understanding of the team's mission  
    - **Rally your team** against a worthy competitor 
        - Most people **get more motivation through competition** than positive encouragement

## Unleashing the Power of Personality Psychology  
* Most of my job as a leader has been focused on relationships:
    - inspiring and helping people **find their motivation**, 
    **consoling** those who feel down about their work,
    and the hardest part of my job, **resolving conflicts** 

* **Empathy** is the key  
    - you must **listen and understand the perspective of others**,
    especially if it's different from yours.
    **Understand why people think what they think and do what they do**.
    - **All behavior is purposeful**. If someone's behavior doesn't make sense to you,
    that's often because you have not taken the time to **explore the underlying reasons for it**  
     

* Big Five traits  
    - extraversion  
    - agreeableness  
    - neuroticism   
    - openness  
    - conscientiousness  

### P14  Read the Room - call on the introverts  
* stop the extroverts and **call on introverts**  
    - apologize for interrupting, summarize the person's point, and pivot  
    - "So, Susanne, excellent points. **Apologies for interrupting**,
    **but we only have ten minutes left** in this meeting. **What you're saying is** ...;
    John, **what do you think**?"  

* listening is a superpower  
    - listen, then **summarize well and beiefly**  

### P15 Agreeableness is a desirable personality trait, being disagreeable sometimes  
* balance agreeableness with the courage to make unpopular yet necessary decisions  

* Agreeableness is **the willingness to go along and get along** - not having to have it your way    
    - if you're too agreeable, some people will take advantage of you.
    There are advantages to **a bit of disagreeableness**.  
    
* be disagreeable about 10% of the time.
    - **learn to pick your battles**  
    - you're pushing people to grow, especially if you're **giving them feedback in a respectful way** that 
    lets them know they're valeud
